Credits pay for model generation including prompt, context/files and output. Paid users can purchase extra credits; exhausted balance pauses generation. Monthly credits roll and expire after 65 days; purchased credits expire after a year and need an active paid plan to use. Team purchased credit pools are shared, monthly entitlements remain individual. [Billing mechanics](https://v0.app/docs/pricing)
## Compute and gotchas
CPU/RAM/disk, execution lifetime, independent agent concurrency and VM-minute tariff are **null**. No proof a v0 subscription grants free Vercel Sandbox usage or production Vercel hosting. Those are separately priced infrastructure products. Don't equate $30 of generation balance to $30 of resource compute.
## Worked examples
One new Plus user pays $30 for $30 monthly generation credit before overage. Fifty Plus users have $1,500 base fees and their separate allowances, but seats do not establish fifty simultaneous 4/8 machines or 8,800 hours. Task tokens/model choice are required for generation cost; app hosting/storage/network costs need a separate deployment model. 50 GiB snapshots and 100 GiB egress cannot be priced from the generation subscription. Regime-only.
