## Caps / gotchas
Agent execution has a **59-minute hard maximum** per session; break larger work into sessions. Included Actions minutes and AI credits can cover their respective meters; exhaustion needs budget/overage enabled. Old premium-request counts are not safe current assumptions after the 2026 usage-based transition. Do not double-charge resource-second cloud sandboxes and Actions for one service, or label Actions runner capacity as the agent's guaranteed hardware. RAM, disk and actual chosen runner for a particular task remain null here. [Agent documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/concepts/agents/coding-agent/about-coding-agent)
## Worked examples
A 30-minute run on a charged baseline 2-core Linux runner costs **$0.18 compute** before included minutes, plus AI credits. 8,800 hours = 528,000 minutes, or $3,168 at this rate before grants—but this is an illustrative **2-core** runner total, not a 4/8 quote, and sessions must be split below 59 minutes. A 4/8-compliant selected runner must be independently priced. Snapshot 50 GiB is not Actions artifact storage; do not substitute the latter. No per-worker 8-hour session is supported. See the existing cloud-sandboxes patch for product separation and expired promotion correction.
Included AI credits reset on the calendar month and do not roll over; variable flex allotments are not permanent guaranteed quotas. Additional model usage draws a USD budget at 100 credits/$1. These credits do not pay the separate Actions-minute meter. [Individual billing](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/concepts/billing/billing-for-individuals)
