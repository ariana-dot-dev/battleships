BYOK removes Warp AI-credit charges, NOT platform or hosted-compute charges. Enterprise self-hosted workers avoid Warp compute credits, but not platform credits or external infrastructure costs. Numeric compute-credits/hour and platform-credits/hour were not found in the fetched current table/docs; leave both null. ## Clock semantics / overage
Platform hours start in the environment including setup, exclude queued time and idle between turns. Warp harness excludes command/tool execution time; Claude Code/Codex harnesses count the entire run, including tests and provider waits. Partial hours proportional. Compute clock is separate. [Platform credits](https://docs.warp.dev/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/platform-credits)
Purchased add-ons last 12 months; auto-reload and spend caps available. After included credits and add-ons run out, cloud runs are blocked unless replenished. [Add-ons](https://docs.warp.dev/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/add-on-credits)
## Examples
Single Max subscriber has the 4/8 shape for $200 base, but no established hours per credit. Fifty concurrent 4/8-minimum workers exceed Max's 20 limit; Business's 80 limit and 8/16 shape fit capacity, but costs still require three measured credit amounts. Formula: subscription + priced add-on credits needed for `(AI + compute + platform) - applicable included pool`. 8,800 wall-hours at 30% CPU do not fix those three values. Disk, retained snapshots and egress null. February launch credits were promotional and must not count as current entitlement.
Add-on docs list 400 credits/$10, 1000/$20, 3000/$50 and 6500/$100. The rounded per-credit labels on that table are not exact ($50/3000=$0.016667). The pricing page distinguishes paid-plan and free-plan purchase rates, so confirm the applicable pack at checkout rather than infer one permanent conversion from included credits.
