These are agent/tool/model credits, not CPU-seconds. For credit-billed general agent tasks, compute vCPU/RAM/disk, session maximum and guaranteed concurrency remain null. Persistent Claw Cloud Computers are a distinct subscription below; do not transfer their specs to every agent task. AI Drive capacity is **not VM RAM or snapshot allowance**. The marketing page guarantees benefits through December 31, 2026; the current Help Center says June 30, 2027 with cohort-specific grandfathering. Preserve this source conflict rather than assume perpetual unlimited use.
## Examples / gotchas
Nominal plan allocations are $0.002499/credit (Plus) and approximately $0.002/credit (Pro), calculated by dividing fee by credits; not standalone credit sale prices. One Plus subscriber pays $24.99 before any extras. Fifty subscriptions would be $1,249.50 base, not proof of fifty concurrent 4/8 agents, 8,800 VM-hours or commercial seat-sharing permission. Hourly normalized cost, retained snapshots and egress remain null.
The primary `/pricing` page was bot-protected/empty even in Chrome; the official localized membership page was readable through web retrieval. Marketing says 12× credits whereas 125,000/10,000 is 12.5; preserve explicit quantities. Do not substitute a desktop Claw running on the user's machine for hosted compute. Regime-only, not zero-cost infrastructure.
## Separate Claw Cloud Computer regime
[Official membership documentation](https://www.genspark.ai/helpcenter/membership-plans) lists Lite **2 vCPU/4 GB/64 GB disk**, Standard **2/8/64**, and Powerful **4/16/128**. Monthly machine prices remain null in retrieved evidence. Cloud subscription includes dedicated infrastructure and fixed IP; idle machine time does not spend task credits, but the flat subscription remains payable. Active messages/tools/schedules/Heartbeat use separate shared credits. Local desktop mode runs on the customer's machine and needs no cloud-computer subscription. [Claw guide](https://www.genspark.ai/helpcenter/genspark-claw)
Powerful meets or exceeds 4/8 hardware, but the 50-worker/month total cannot be calculated without machine subscription price, allowed instance count, and AI usage. Cancellation reclaims computer and deletes data. Public price-config API confirms $24.99/$249.99 monthly and $239.99/$2399.99 annual Plus/Pro; it exposes $20/$40 credit-pack price fields but no verified pack quantities, so no universal overage $/credit is inferred.
